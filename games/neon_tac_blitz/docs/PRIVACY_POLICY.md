# Privacy Policy — Neon Tac Blitz

**Last updated:** 2026-11-13  
**App:** Neon Tac Blitz (`com.dhayalram.neontacblitz`)

## Summary

Offline single-player game vs AI. Wins, streaks, and daily gift progress are stored **only on your device**. No accounts, ads, or analytics in this build.

## On-device data

| Data | Purpose | Leaves device? |
|---|---|---|
| Wins / losses / best streak / gems | Gameplay | No |
| Daily claim day / login streak | Daily gift | No |

## Not collected

Personal info, location, contacts, ads ID, analytics.

## Permissions

- **Vibrate** — haptic feedback only.

No `INTERNET` permission in this release.
